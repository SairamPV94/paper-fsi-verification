## Conclusions
- **No defect found in how no-slip is enforced, in the traction sign or orientation, or in the alpha timing.**
- **The one structural suspect** is item 3: fixed-w interface nodes drop the fluid reaction that the weak form would pass to the ring. It cannot be classed as a defect: the continuum limit is correct, the discrepancy is an O(h) interface term, and the observed energy creation (Gamma 1.4e-2 in G1) has not been tied to it. Round-1 runs E1 and E2 (wall-only versus interface-only no-slip) discriminate this directly.
- A software-defect claim would need a minimal reproducer showing a consistent-reaction mismatch of the predicted size, plus a regression test; none exists yet.
